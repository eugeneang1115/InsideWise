Insidewise privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Applies to the Insidewise website and Android app.

Insidewise lets people read and write anonymous reviews of employers in Malaysia. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Reviews you write: ratings, headline, text, employment status (current or former) and language. Reviews are shown publicly without your name or email.
  • Companies you add: name, industry, city, and optional size and website.
  • Reports and appeals you send about reviews.
  • Sign-in session: a random token that keeps you signed in, stored as a cookie on the website or in app storage on Android.

Our server logs record only the type of request, the page address and the result. They do not record your IP address or account. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived network logs for security and reliability.

Who can see what

Everyone can read published reviews, which never show who wrote them. We keep a private link between each review and its author's account so you can edit or delete your reviews and so we can deal with abuse. That link is never shown publicly or to moderators. Moderators see review text, ratings and reports, but not the author's email.

Advertising (Android app only)

The Android app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on company pages. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings under Google, Ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The website shows no ads.

Maps

Maps are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which receives map requests from your device, including its IP address. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our hosting providers, Render and Neon, in Singapore. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell your data.

Export or delete your data

Sign in on the website or the app, open My reviews, and choose Download my data or Delete account. Deleting your account permanently removes your email, password, sessions and every review you wrote, straight away. Companies you added stay listed but are no longer linked to you.

Children

Insidewise is meant for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. For privacy questions, contact us using the email address on our Google Play listing.
